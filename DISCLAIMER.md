# Disclaimer

**Not financial advice.** This repository and its page are for information and entertainment only.
Nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security, or to follow any trader.

**Unofficial and unverified.** The numbers are calculated automatically from orders the traders post
publicly on the tastylive Follow Feed and Lossdog VIP. Neither feed reports P&L, so every figure
here is an estimate. It can be incomplete or wrong because of:

- missed or delayed posts
- fills that differ from the posted price
- fees and commissions, which are not included
- expired options settled at the day's closing price, which ignores early assignment and
  after-hours moves
- the scoring rules described in the [README](README.md)

**Not anyone's real account.** These are not brokerage statements. They do not show what any trader
or follower actually made or lost. Lossdog results are per one unit, not a real position size.

**Past results do not predict future results.** Options and futures trading carries substantial
risk of loss and is not suitable for everyone.

**No affiliation.** This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by tastylive,
Lossdog or any trader listed. Trader names and notes appear as the traders posted them publicly.

**Corrections and removal.** To report an error, or to ask for your name to be removed, open an
[issue](https://github.com/joncovington/trader-scoreboard/issues).
