Trader Scoreboard

Unofficial results, calculated automatically from public trade posts. They may be incomplete or wrong, and they are not financial advice. Full disclaimer.

How positions are scored

  • Every order a trader places on the same contracts — opens, adds, rolls, partial closes — is one position. Its P&L is the sum of their net cash once every leg is flat.
  • tastylive is in actual dollars at the trader's size. Lossdog publishes no size, so it is per one unit.
  • Untracked: closes of positions opened before tracking began — counted, never priced.
  • Expired: options that expired without a closing order wait, out of P&L, for settlement prices.
  • The period filters closed positions by close date; open, expired and untracked are as of now.
